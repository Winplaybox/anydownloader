Understanding Self-XSS & Developer Console Security

If you opened your browser's Developer Tools (Console) and saw a large red "Stop!" message, do not panic. That notice is an automated defensive warning designed to protect your accounts and sensitive credentials from cyber scams known as Self-XSS (Self Cross-Site Scripting).

Developer Console Output (Preview)
Stop!
This is a browser feature intended for developers. If someone told you to copy and paste something here to enable an OVD feature or "hack" someone's account, it is a scam and will give them unauthorized access to your account.
See https://ovd.winplaybox.in/security for more information.

What is Self-XSS and How Does the Scam Work?

The browser Developer Console allows programmers to test code directly within the web page. Because code executed in the console runs with your full browser privileges, attackers frequently exploit users who do not understand technical scripting:

1. The Bait
A scammer or video promises a "secret script to unlock private Instagram stories, private Facebook posts, or get unlimited game credits."
2. The Trap
They ask you to press F12 or Ctrl+Shift+I, navigate to the "Console" tab, and paste a block of obfuscated JavaScript.
3. The Theft
The moment you press Enter, their script steals your active login cookies, session tokens, and passwords, sending them straight to the attacker's server.
